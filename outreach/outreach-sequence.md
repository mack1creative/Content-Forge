# Outreach Email Sequence
## SaaS Companies - Cold Email

---

## Email 1: The Hook

**Subject:** Quick question about [Company]'s content

Hi [First Name],

I'm reaching out because I've been following [Company]'s content, and honestly — you guys are doing some solid work.

But I'll be direct: I noticed you've been scaling your [blog/social/email], and I curious — do you have enough bandwidth to keep up with demand?

We're Mack Content Agency, and we help SaaS companies like yours scale content without the in-house headache.

Curious — is content something you're looking to offload anytime soon?

Best,
Mack

---

## Email 2: The Follow-Up (3 days later)

**Subject:** Not sure if this is a fit, but...

Hi [First Name],

Kept thinking about your content setup. Here's what I'll say:

Most companies we talk to fall into one of two buckets:
1. They have content, but it's inconsistent
2. They don't have time to produce enough

Which one sounds more like [Company]?

If it's neither — no worries, just wanted to put this on your radar.

If it hits — let's chat. 15 minutes, no pitch. Just benchmarking.

Best,
Mack

---

## Email 3: The Value Add (5 days later)

**Subject:** Something for you (no catch)

Hi [First Name],

Last one, I promise.

I put together a quick content audit for [Company] — looked at your top 5 blog posts and identified what's working and what's missing.

Key takeaways:
- Your posts on [topic] are getting traction
- You're missing content around [opportunity]
- Your SEO could be stronger on [keyword]

No catch — just wanted to show you what we'd do if we worked together.

Worth 15 minutes of your time to walk through?

Best,
Mack

---

## Email 4: The Close (7 days later)

**Subject:** Last check — content help?

Hi [First Name],

I know — I've been persistent. Here's why:

Content is the one thing that compounds over time. The companies doing it right are building moats their competitors can't touch.

If you're set on content, let's talk. If you're not — just say so and I'll stop reaching out.

Either way, best of luck with [Company].

Best,
Mack

---

## Quick-Reply Responses

**If they reply "interested":**
> Great! Let's schedule 15 minutes here: [Calendly link]. I'll send over a brief questionnaire beforehand so we make the most of our time.

**If they reply "not interested":**
> Totally understand. Thanks for the transparency. If things change or you know another founder who needs content help, I'm here. Best of luck with [Company]!

**If they reply "who are you":**
> Fair question. I'm Mack — I run a content agency focused on SaaS companies. We write blogs, emails, social — basically help founders scale content without the in-house team. Happy to share more or jump on a quick call.
