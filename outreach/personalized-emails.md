**If they respond "not interested":**
> Totally understand. Thanks for the transparency. If things change, I'm here. Best of luck with Comp!

**If they don't respond (follow-up after 3 days):**
> Hi [Name], just following up on my last email. No pressure if content isn't a priority right now — I just wanted to put this on your radar. Let me know if you'd ever like to chat.

## Notes

- Keep emails short (under 150 words)
- Reference their specific news/funding
- Ask a question that makes them think
- No attachments in first email
- Always offer to jump on a quick call
