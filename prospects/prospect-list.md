## Prospecting Strategy

**Target Companies:**
- Recently funded (Seed, Series A, Series B)
- In growth mode (need content to fuel growth)
- Have budget for marketing services
- Likely to need ongoing content support

**Ideal Client Profile:**
- $1M+ ARR
- Recently raised funding
- 20-100 employees
- Actively hiring for marketing/growth roles

## Categories to Focus On

1. **AI/ML SaaS** - Highest funding, most need content
2. **Fintech** - Well-funded, content-heavy
3. **HR Tech** - Growing niche, B2B focus
4. **Construction Tech** - Underexplored, good rates

## Outreach Priority

1. **Series A companies** - Have funding, need to scale content
2. **Series B companies** - Scaling fast, likely need more hands
3. **Well-funded Seed** - Building foundation, great long-term clients

## Notes

- Focus on companies that recently raised (last 6 months)
- Check LinkedIn for marketing leads to contact
- Use the "recently funded" angle in outreach
- Target companies with clear content needs (growing social, blog, etc.)
