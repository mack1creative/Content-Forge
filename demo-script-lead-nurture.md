```

AI: "Hi Sarah! This is Mack with [Team Name]. I saw you're looking at homes in Clermont under $300k. Are you still in the market?"

AI: "That's great! What matters most to you in your next home — square footage, location, or price?"

Sarah: "I want at least 3 bedrooms and I need a garage. My budget is around $275k."
```
