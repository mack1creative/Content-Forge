## Cost Structure

### Fixed Costs (per month, assuming 100 agents)
| Cost | Amount |
|------|--------|
| AI/llm (OpenAI API) | ~$1,500 |
| Twilio (phone calls/SMS) | ~$800 |
| Hosting/server | ~$200 |
| Email delivery (SendGrid) | ~$100 |
| **Total Fixed Costs** | **~$2,600** |

### Variable Costs (per lead)
| Cost | Amount |
|------|--------|
| AI processing | ~$0.02/lead |
| SMS (if sent) | ~$0.02/lead |
| Phone minutes | ~$0.05/lead |
| **Total Variable** | **~$0.09/lead** |

---

## Profit Margins

### Per Agent (Professional Tier - $249/mo)
- Revenue: $249/month
- Variable cost (~100 leads): ~$9
- **Gross Margin: $240/agent/month**
- **Gross Margin %: 96.4%**

### At Scale (100 Agents)
- Monthly Revenue: $24,900
- Fixed Costs: $2,600
- Variable Costs: $900
- **Net Profit: $21,400/month**
- **Annual Profit: $256,800**

---

## Unit Economics

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
| Channel | Cost |
|---------|------|
| Cold outreach (email) | $15-25/lead |
| Facebook/Instagram ads | $50-100/lead |
| Referrals | $0 (bonus only) |
| SEO/content | $200-500/month |

**Target CAC:** $50 or less
**Payback period:** 2-3 months

### Lifetime Value (LTV)
- Average contract length: 18 months
- Average LTV: $249 × 18 = **$4,482**
- LTV:CAC ratio (at $50 CAC): **90:1** ✅
