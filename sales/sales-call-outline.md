# SaaS Content Agency - Sales Call Outline

## Pre-Call Prep
- [ ] Pull prospect's website and recent content
- [ ] Check if they've been mentioned in news
- [ ] Prepare 2 customized talking points

---

## 1. Opener (30 seconds)

> "Hey [Name], thanks for making time to chat. I know you're busy, so I'll keep this tight.
> 
> Quick background: We help SaaS companies scale their content output without the overhead of building an in-house team. We're a dedicated content partner — we write, you approve. Simple.
> 
> How's content going on your end right now?"

---

## 2. Discovery (2-3 minutes)

Ask these questions to qualify:

1. **Current State:**
   - "How are you handling content today — in-house team, freelancers, or just getting by?"
   - "What's your biggest content challenge right now?"

2. **Goals:**
   - "What's the goal for the next 6 months? More traffic? Lead gen? Brand awareness?"
   - "How will you measure success?"

3. **Budget:**
   - "What's the budget range you're working with for content?" (Don't push hard on this yet)

4. **Decision:**
   - " who's involved in the decision-making process?"

---

## 3. Pitch (1 minute)

> "Here's how we work: We become your dedicated content team. You tell us what you need — blogs, emails, case studies, social — and we deliver.
> 
> We don't waste your time with back-and-forth revisions. You get clean, publish-ready content.
> 
> Most clients see results within 90 days — better traffic, more leads, or just a consistently growing content library."

---

## 4. Objection Handling

### "How much is this?"
> "We tailor to each client based on volume and needs. Before I quote a number, can you share what's your approximate monthly content needs — maybe 5 blogs, 10 social posts, that kind of thing?"

### "I need to think about it"
> "Totally understand. What's the biggest factor — budget, timing, or do you want to see more proof? If it's proof, I can send over some case studies in your niche."

### "We already have a writer/freelancer"
> "Great, that means you see the value. What's working well? What would you improve if you could? [Listen] Yeah, we hear that a lot — consistency is hard. That's exactly what we solve."

### "Can you just write one piece first?"
> "We can do a trial. One piece, paid, so you can see the quality. If you like it, we move to a monthly retainer. No pressure."

### "This seems expensive"
> "I hear you. When you look at the cost of a full-time writer (salary + benefits + overhead) plus the management time, we're actually 40-50% less. And you get a team, not just one person."

---

## 5. Close (30 seconds)

> "Sounds like a fit. Here's what I'd recommend: Let's start with a trial. You get [X] content pieces for $[Y], and you see firsthand how we work.
> 
> No long-term contract. If it's not working, you walk away. But I'm confident you'll want to continue.
> 
> Next step: I'll send over a personalized proposal — can you review by [Day]?"
