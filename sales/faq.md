# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### Who are you?
We're a dedicated content agency built for SaaS companies. We handle all your content end-to-end — blogs, emails, case studies, social posts, whitepapers. You focus on approval and strategy.

### How is this different from hiring a freelancer?
A freelancer is one person with one skillset. We're a content system — you get consistent quality, faster turnaround, and multiple content types handled simultaneously. No vacation, no sick days, no turnover risk.

### Do you specialize in any industries?
We focus on SaaS — B2B and B2C. We've written for companies in analytics, cybersecurity, HR tech, marketing automation, and more. We'll research your niche before writing.

## Pricing

### How much do you charge?
Our retainer model starts at $2,000/month for 8-10 pieces of content. Custom packages available based on your needs.

### What's included?
Each package includes:
- Writing by our team
- One round of revisions
- SEO optimization (where applicable)
- Delivery in your preferred format (Google Doc, CMS upload, etc.)

### Do you offer one-off projects?
Yes, but at a premium rate. We prefer retainers because they produce better results for both sides — we learn your brand, you get consistent output.

### Are there long-term contracts?
No. We work month-to-month. You can pause or cancel anytime with 14 days notice.

## Process

### How does it work?
1. We onboard — share your brand guidelines, past content, and goals
2. You give us topics/briefs (or we research them for you)
3. We write and deliver
4. You approve or request changes
5. Repeat

### How fast do you deliver?
First draft within 48-72 hours of brief approval. Revisions within 24 hours.

### Who writes the content?
Our team of experienced writers with SaaS backgrounds. You'll have a dedicated point of contact.

### What if I don't like the content?
One round of revisions is included. If it's truly not hitting the mark after that, we'll rewrite until it's right.
