Frequently Asked Questions

How does billing work?

We bill monthly, in advance. All packages are month-to-month — no long-term contracts. Pause or cancel anytime with 14 days notice.

What if I need more content?

No problem. We can add one-off projects at our standard per-piece rates, or upgrade your monthly package at any time.

How do revisions work?

Each package includes one round of revisions on all content. If you need major changes after that, we bill additional time at our standard hourly rate.

Can I try before committing?

Absolutely. Start with a one-off project to test our work. If you love it (and we think you will), we can convert to a monthly package.

Who writes the content?

Our team of experienced writers with backgrounds in B2B SaaS. You'll work with a dedicated team that learns your brand voice and preferences over time.

What's included in case studies?

We handle everything — customer interview, story arc, writing, and final draft. We only need access to your customer for a 30-minute call.
