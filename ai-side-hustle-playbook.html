1. Prompt Engineering for Clients

The Opportunity

Businesses are using ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini but getting mediocre results. They need someone who knows how to write prompts that actually work — and they're willing to pay for it.

What You Do

You write custom prompts for specific business needs: customer service auto-responders, sales email generators, content brief creators, data analysis scripts.

How to Start 1. Pick an industry (real estate, e-commerce, SaaS)
2. Create 3-5 sample prompts for that industry
3. Reach out to businesses on LinkedIn or cold email
4. Offer a "prompt audit" as a low-ticket entry point ($50-150)

Pricing

Prompt packages: $100-500 | Ongoing retainers: $500-2,000/month

Cold Email Template:
"Hi [Name], I help [industry] companies get 10x better results from ChatGPT by writing custom prompts tailored to their workflow. Recent example: [specific result].

Want me to show you what's possible? Book a 15-min call: [calendar link]"

2. AI Content Writing

The Opportunity

Every business needs content — blog posts, emails, landing pages, product descriptions. But most business owners don't have the time or skill to write it themselves. AI makes you 5-10x faster.

What You Do

You write content using AI as your assistant. The key difference: you know how to prompt, edit, and humanize the output so it doesn't sound robotic.

How to Start 1. Build a portfolio (write 3-5 sample pieces)
2. Create a service page on your website
3. Post on Twitter/LinkedIn about AI writing tips
4. Join Upwork, Fiverr, or apply to content agencies

Pricing

Blog posts: $100-300 each | Email sequences: $200-500 | Landing pages: $300-1,000

"I went from charging $50 per blog post to $250 once I started showing clients the AI workflow and the results it produced. They pay for outcomes, not words." — Sarah, freelance writer

3. Automation Consulting

The Opportunity

Small businesses waste hours on repetitive tasks: data entry, follow-up emails, appointment scheduling. Most don't even know it's solvable. You show them.

What You Do

You audit their workflow, find the time-wasters, and build simple automations using tools like Zapier, Make, or even just clever prompt setups.

How to Start 1. Learn one tool deeply (Zapier is most popular)
2. Audit your own workflow first — automate something small
3. Create case studies showing time saved
4. Reach out to local businesses or post on行业 forums

Pricing

Audits: $200-500 | Implementation: $500-3,000 | Retainers: $300-1,500/month

4. AI Social Media Management

The Opportunity

Business owners know they "should" be on social media but don't have the bandwidth. AI makes it possible to manage 5-10 clients instead of 1-2.

What You Do

You create content, schedule posts, and engage with their audience. The AI handles the heavy lifting — you provide strategy and human touch.

How to Start 1. Pick 1-2 platforms (LinkedIn + X is a good combo)
2. Build a few sample content calendars
3. Offer a "starter package" to get your first client
4. Use scheduling tools (Buffer, Later, Postwise)

Pricing

Starter packages: $300-500/month | Full management: $800-2,500/month

Content Calendar Framework (Weekly):
• Monday: Value post (tip, insight, how-to)
• Tuesday: Client result or testimonial
• Wednesday: Behind-the-scenes or personal story
• Thursday: Engagement post (question, poll)
• Friday: Weekend-relevant or light content

6. YouTube Script Writing

The Opportunity

YouTubers need scripts — especially faceless creators and educational channels. The market is huge and AI makes it fast.

What You Do

You write video scripts that hook viewers, deliver value, and keep them watching. AI helps research and draft; you add the human polish.

How to Start 1. Write 3-5 sample scripts in different niches
2. Post on Upwork, Fiverr, or reach out to YouTubers directly
3. Offer "script + hooks" packages
4. Build relationships with growing creators

Pricing

Per-script: $50-300 | Monthly retainers: $500-2,000

Script Hook Template:
"Most people think [common misconception]. But the truth is [truth]. In this video, I'm going to show you [outcome] in [timeframe] — without [common barrier]."

7. AI-Powered Copywriting

The Opportunity

Copywriting is one of the highest-paid skills in marketing. With AI, you can write twice as fast at half the effort — while charging premium rates.

What You Do

Sales pages, landing pages, Facebook ads, email sequences — you write the persuasive words that make people buy.

Pricing

Per project: $500-5,000 | Retainers: $2,000-10,000/month

"The first thing I tell clients: I use AI to do in 2 hours what used to take me 8. That doesn't lower the price — it raises the value." — Lisa, direct response copywriter

9. SaaS Tool Building (No Code Needed)

The Opportunity

You don't need to code to build simple tools anymore. Bolt, Lovable, and similar platforms let you ship in hours, not months.

What You Do

Find a specific pain point, build a simple tool to solve it, charge for access. Could be a prompt manager, a content repurposer, a meeting summarizer.

How to Start 1. Talk to 5-10 people in your target market
2. Find the most common pain point
3. Build a simple version in a weekend (Bolt.new is free)
4. Launch on Product Hunt, Twitter, relevant communities

Pricing

Free tier + $9-29/month Pro | Lifetime deals: $47-197

"I built a Chrome extension that summarizes YouTube videos in 30 seconds. Cost me $0 to build. Made $1,200 in the first month." — Dev, indie hacker
