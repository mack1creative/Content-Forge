# Chapter 10: AI Consulting & Strategy — The Complete Guide

## The Opportunity

Companies want to "do AI" but don't know where to start. They hear about ChatGPT, Claude, and AI tools but can't figure out:
- Which tools to use
- How to implement them
- What processes to automate
- How to train their team

That's where you come in. As an AI consultant, you assess their needs, recommend solutions, and build implementation roadmaps.

This is premium work. You're not just doing tasks — you're advising on strategy. Rates reflect that: $500-2,000 for audits, $2,000-10,000/month for retainers.

## Step-by-Step

### Phase 1: Build Deep Expertise (Weeks 1-4)
- Master 2-3 AI tools deeply
- Learn prompt engineering
- Understand business workflows
- Study AI in your target industry

### Phase 2: Create Your Offer (Week 5)
- Audit: $500-2,000
- Strategy session: $300-1,000
- Implementation: $1,000-5,000
- Retainer: $2,000-10,000/month

### Phase 3: Find Clients (Weeks 6-10)
- LinkedIn thought leadership
- Cold outreach
- Referrals from existing clients
- Business networking events

### Phase 4: Deliver (Ongoing)

**Audit Framework:**
1. Current workflow mapping
2. Time-waster identification  
3. AI solution recommendations
4. Implementation roadmap (90-day)
5. Expected ROI calculation
