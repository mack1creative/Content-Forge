# Chapter 10: AI Consulting & Strategy

Companies want to "do AI" but don't know where to start. They hear about ChatGPT and AI tools but can't figure out which tools to use, how to implement them, what to automate, or how to train their team.

That's where you come in. As an AI consultant, you assess their needs, recommend solutions, and build implementation roadmaps.

This is premium work. You're advising on strategy, not just doing tasks. Rates reflect that: $500-2,000 for audits, $2,000-10,000/month for retainers.

## Getting Started

### Weeks 1-4: Build Deep Expertise

Master 2-3 AI tools deeply. Learn prompt engineering. Understand business workflows. Study AI in your target industry.

### Week 5: Create Your Offer

- Audit: $500-2,000
- Strategy session: $300-1,000
- Implementation: $1,000-5,000
- Retainer: $2,000-10,000/month

### Weeks 6-10: Find Clients

- LinkedIn thought leadership
- Cold outreach
- Referrals from existing clients
- Business networking events

### Ongoing: Deliver

**Audit Framework:**

1. Current workflow mapping
2. Time-waster identification
3. AI solution recommendations
4. Implementation roadmap (90-day)
5. Expected ROI calculation

## Tools

- ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini
- Zapier, Make
- Process documentation tools
- Project management tools

## 90-Day Plan

**Days 1-28:** Master tools deeply, study industry.

**Days 29-35:** Create offers and pricing.

**Days 36-70:** Send outreach, get first clients.

**Days 71-90:** Deliver audits, pitch retainers, build case studies.

This is one of the highest-paying AI hustles. Companies need strategic help, not just tool recommendations. If you can think strategically and communicate well, this is a great fit.
