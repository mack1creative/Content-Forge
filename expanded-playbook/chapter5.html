Chapter 5: Course Creation & Digital Products

Create once, sell infinitely — the holy grail of AI side hustles

This is the holy grail. Create something once, sell it infinitely.

Unlike services where you trade time for money, digital products generate revenue while you sleep. A course you build in one month can make money for years.

And AI makes creation way faster. You can use it to brainstorm, write scripts, create worksheets, generate quizzes.

Getting Started

Weeks 1-2: Find Your Topic

Pick something where you have genuine expertise and people are actively looking for solutions.

Validate by checking:

  • Google search volume
  • Existing courses on the topic
  • Problem intensity — how painful is it?

Weeks 3-4: Validate First

Before building, prove people will pay:

  • Build an email list (even 50-100 people)
  • Create a free lead magnet
  • Survey your audience: "Would you pay for a course on X?"

Weeks 5-10: Build the Course

Structure:

  • Introduction
  • 4-5 modules, 3-5 lessons each
  • Total: 20-30 lessons, 2-4 hours of content

Use AI for first drafts, then heavily edit for your voice.

Weeks 11-12: Launch

Week 1: Soft launch to email list

Week 2: Full launch, social media, last chance

Pricing

PDF Guide: $27-47

Video Course: $97-197

Template Pack: $47-97

Membership: $19-49/month

90-Day Plan

Days 1-14: Pick topic, research demand, build email list.

Days 15-30: Create lead magnet, pre-sell.

Days 31-60: Create course, set up platform.

Days 61-90: Launch, get testimonials, plan course #2.

Bottom Line

Course creation is high-leverage. Even if it doesn't become your main income, it establishes authority and creates recurring revenue.
