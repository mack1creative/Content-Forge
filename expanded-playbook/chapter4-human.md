# Chapter 4: AI Social Media Management

Business owners know they need to be on social media. They know consistency matters. But they don't have the time to post every day, engage with every comment, and figure out what the algorithms want.

That's where you come in.

This isn't about replacing the human element with AI. It's about using AI to handle the heavy lifting — content creation, scheduling, research — while you provide the strategy and engagement that builds real community.

With AI, you can manage 10 clients effectively instead of 2-3. That means better margins and more revenue.

## Getting Started

### Weeks 1-2: Build Your Foundation

Pick one or two platforms and actually understand them:

- **LinkedIn** — B2B, professionals, thought leadership
- **Instagram** — Visual brands, younger audience
- **TikTok** — Younger audience, viral potential
- **Twitter/X** — News, opinions, real-time

Learn the basics: content strategy, writing for each platform, basic design (Canva), engagement, analytics.

### Week 3: Choose Your Niche

Don't try to serve everyone. Pick one niche and become known for it.

| Niche | Platform Priority |
|-------|-------------------|
| Real Estate | LinkedIn, Instagram |
| Coaches | LinkedIn, Twitter |
| E-commerce | Instagram, TikTok |
| Local Services | Facebook |
| Health/Wellness | Instagram, TikTok |
| B2B/SaaS | LinkedIn |

Pick one platform and own it.

### Weeks 4-6: Build Your Portfolio

- Run your own accounts first
- Offer free work for a favorite brand in exchange for testimonial rights
- Create speculative content for real businesses

### Weeks 7-10: Get Clients

**Cold email:**

"Hey [Name],

I help [niche] companies build their social media without spending hours on it themselves.

Most business owners know they 'should' be posting more. They just don't have the time. I handle strategy, content, scheduling, engagement — the whole thing.

Results from recent clients:
- Grew followers 340% in 90 days
- Generated 200+ leads in 60 days
- Increased engagement 280%

Book a call if interested: [link]"

**Where to find work:**

- Direct outreach
- LinkedIn posting
- Upwork, Fiverr

### Ongoing: Deliver

Services:

- Starter ($300-500/month) — 3 posts/week, 1 platform, basic engagement
- Growth ($800-1,500/week) — 5 posts/week, 2 platforms, full engagement
- Premium ($1,500-2,500/week) — Daily posting, 3+ platforms, strategy calls

## Tools

- Buffer, Later, Hootsuite — Scheduling
- Canva — Graphics
- ChatGPT, Copy.ai — Content drafts
- Platform analytics

## Mistakes

- Taking on too many platforms
- Not establishing clear brand guidelines
- Ignoring engagement
- Undercharging
- Not reporting results

## 90-Day Plan

**Days 1-14:** Choose platform, study top creators, set up your profiles.

**Days 15-30:** Create sample content calendar, set pricing, build portfolio.

**Days 31-60:** Send outreach, get first clients.

**Days 61-90:** Raise rates, build waitlist, create referral system.

Social media management is straightforward. The key is specializing in one platform and one niche, then scaling from there.
