Chapter 3: Automation Consulting

Connect the tools, save hours every week, get paid for it

Every small business has the same problem: too many repetitive tasks and not enough time. Data entry, follow-up emails, appointment scheduling, invoice sending. The list goes on. Most business owners just accept it as part of running a business.

That's your opportunity.

Automation is about finding those repetitive tasks, connecting tools together so they handle themselves, and saving hours every week. You don't write code — you use platforms like Zapier and Make to connect apps that already exist.

The ROI is immediate. Save a business owner 5 hours per week, that's 260 hours per year. At $50/hour, that's $13,000 in value. Charging $500-2,000 for the setup is a no-brainer for them.

Getting Started

Weeks 1-3: Learn the Tools

You don't need to code, but you need to be fluent in automation tools:

Zapier — Most popular. Triggers and actions. Easy to learn.

Make (formerly Integromat) — More powerful, better for complex stuff.

Google Workspace — Sheets, Gmail, Calendar automation.

Practice projects:

  • Connect Google Form to Google Sheet
  • Set up a Zap that adds new email subscribers to a spreadsheet
  • Create a workflow that saves Gmail attachments to Dropbox
  • Automate social media posting

Week 4: Pick Your Market

Focus on clients who have repetitive processes, use multiple software tools, and have budget to improve.

Good targets:

  • Real estate agents — lead follow-up, showing scheduling
  • E-commerce — order processing, notifications, inventory
  • Agencies — client onboarding, invoicing
  • Coaches — booking, intake forms, follow-ups
  • Local services — job scheduling, estimates, reminders

Real estate and e-commerce are great starting points. Lots of pain, lots of budget.
