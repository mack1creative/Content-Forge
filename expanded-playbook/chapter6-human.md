# Chapter 6: YouTube Script Writing

YouTube is huge. Every day, people search for how-tos, explanations, reviews. But most creators are terrible at writing scripts. They know their topic, but structuring it into a compelling video is hard.

That's where you come in.

YouTube scripts follow predictable structures. Once you master the format, you can write a script in 30-45 minutes that used to take hours.

## The Script Structure

Every good YouTube video follows this pattern:

**The Hook (first 30 seconds):**
- Address a specific problem or desire
- Promise a clear outcome
- Create curiosity

**The Problem:** Why should viewers care? Make it relatable.

**The Solution:** Your main content. 3-5 key points.

**The Transformation:** Show results, case studies.

**The CTA:** Like, subscribe, comment, watch another video.

## Getting Started

### Weeks 1-2: Learn the Format

Watch 20 videos in your niche. Notice how they structure hooks, content, CTAs. Practice writing hooks for the same topic.

### Week 3: Pick a Niche

Good niches: Tech reviews, tutorials, true crime, finance, health/fitness, gaming.

Specialize. Become known as "the script writer for [niche] channels."

### Weeks 4-6: Build Portfolio

Write 5 sample scripts. Include performance data if possible.

### Weeks 7-10: Find Clients

**Outreach:**

"Hey [Name],

I'm a YouTube script writer who specializes in [niche]. I help creators turn their expertise into scripts that hook viewers and keep them watching.

Would you be open to a quick call? I'd love to show you a custom script outline for your next video — no obligation."
