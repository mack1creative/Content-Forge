# Chapter 7: AI-Powered Copywriting

Copywriting is the highest-paid writing. It's not about pretty words — it's about words that sell. Every landing page, sales email, and ad needs copy that converts.

With AI, you can produce high-converting copy faster. Use AI for drafts and research, then apply your copywriting expertise to make it persuasive.

This is premium work. While content writers charge $100-300 per post, copywriters charge $500-5,000+ per project.

## Core Frameworks

**AIDA:** Attention, Interest, Desire, Action — Grab attention, build interest, create desire, drive action.

**PAS:** Problem, Agitation, Solution — Identify the problem, make it feel urgent, present your solution.

**4 Ps:** Picture, Promise, Proof, Push — Paint a picture, make a promise, provide proof, push to action.

## Getting Started

### Weeks 1-3: Learn the Fundamentals

Read these books:

- "The Ultimate Sales Letter" by Dan Kennedy
- "Copywriting Secrets" by Jim Edwards
- "Made to Stick" by Chip & Dan Heath

Study real sales pages, email sequences, Facebook ads.

### Week 4: Choose Your Focus

Specialties:

- Sales pages ($500-5,000)
- Email sequences ($200-2,000)
- Facebook ads ($150-500/ad)
- Product descriptions ($50-200/product)

Pick one. Be the "sales page writer for e-commerce" not "copywriter."

### Weeks 5-7: Build Portfolio

Write spec sales letters for hypothetical products. Offer free work for testimonials.

### Weeks 8-12: Find Clients

Target businesses with poor copy (easy to improve). Post in marketing communities. Apply to agencies.
