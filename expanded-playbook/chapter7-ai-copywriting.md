# Chapter 7: AI-Powered Copywriting — The Complete Guide

## The Opportunity

Copywriting is the highest-paid form of writing. It's not about pretty words — it's about words that sell. Every landing page, sales email, advertisement, and product page needs copy that converts. And most business owners can't write it themselves.

With AI, you can produce high-converting copy faster than ever. Use AI for first drafts, research, and structure, then apply your copywriting expertise to make it persuasive.

This is a premium skill. While content writers might charge $100-300 per blog post, copywriters charge $500-5,000+ per project. The ROI is clearer: you can point to exact revenue increases from your work.

## What Copywriting Is (And Isn't)

**It IS:**
- Persuasive writing that drives action
- Focused on specific outcomes
- About benefits, not features
- Testing and iterating

**It ISN'T:**
- Creative writing or journalism
- Generic content
- About being clever
- One-and-done

## Core Frameworks to Know

### AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action)
The oldest copywriting framework:
- Grab attention with a hook
- Build interest with relevant info
- Create desire by showing benefits
- Drive action with a clear CTA

### PAS (Problem, Agitation, Solution)
- Identify the problem
- Make it feel urgent/painful
- Present your solution

### The 4 Ps (Picture, Promise, Proof, Push)
- Paint a picture of the problem/solution
- Make a promise
- Provide proof (testimonials, data)
- Push to action

### PAS (Problem, Agitation, Solution)
- Identify the problem
- Agitate it — make it worse
- Present the solution

## Step-by-Step: Building Your AI-Powered Copywriting Business

### Phase 1: Learn Copywriting Fundamentals (Weeks 1-3)

**Books to Read:**
- "The Ultimate Sales Letter" by Dan Kennedy
- "Copywriting Secrets" by Jim Edwards
- "Made to Stick" by Chip & Dan Heath
- "Ogilvy on Advertising" by David Ogilvy

**Study Real Copy:**
- Landings page examples
- Sales email sequences
- Facebook ads
- Direct mail letters

**Practice Frameworks:**
Write the same pitch using AIDA, PAS, and 4 Ps. See how different frameworks create different results.

### Phase 2: Choose Your Focus (Week 4)

**Copywriting Specialties:**

| Type | What You Write | Rates |
|------|----------------|-------|
| Sales Pages | Long-form landing pages | $500-5,000 |
| Email Sequences | Automated email campaigns | $200-2,000 |
| Facebook Ads | Ad copy | $150-500/ad |
| Product Descriptions | E-commerce product copy | $50-200/product |
| Case Studies | Client success stories | $300-1,000 |
| Video Sales Letters | Webinar/VSL scripts | $500-3,000 |

**Choose One:**
Specialize first. Then expand. "I write sales pages for e-commerce brands" is better than "I write copy."

### Phase 3: Build Portfolio (Weeks 5-7)

**How to Build Samples:**
1. Rewrite existing sales pages (with permission)
2. Write spec sales letters for hypothetical products
3. Offer free work for nonprofit or friend's business
4. Create your own product and sell it (proves you believe in your own advice)

### Phase 4: Find Clients (Weeks 8-12)

**Outreach:**
- Target businesses with poor copy (easy to improve)
- Post in marketing communities
- Apply to agencies

**Freelance Platforms:**
- Contently (high-quality clients)
- nDash (curated)
- Upwork (volume)

### Phase 5: Deliver and Scale (Ongoing)

**Service Packages:**
- Single project: $500-5,000
- Retainer: $2,000-10,000/month
