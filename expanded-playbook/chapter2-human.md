# Chapter 2: AI Content Writing

Every business needs content. Blog posts, emails, website copy, product descriptions, social posts — the list never ends. Most business owners know they need it, but they don't have time to write it themselves.

That's where you come in.

This isn't about letting AI do all the work. It's about using AI as your tool while you bring the strategy, editing, and human touch that makes content actually connect. The magic is in the combination: AI handles the heavy lifting, you make it feel like a real person wrote it.

## Why It Works

The content market is huge. Companies need more content than ever, but good writers are hard to find. AI makes you 5-10x faster — but the quality still depends on you.

Here's the thing: AI-written content often sounds robotic. It lacks personality. That's where you add value. You're not competing with AI — you're using it to compete with writers who aren't leveraging these tools.

## Getting Started

### Weeks 1-2: Learn the Craft

You need to understand what makes content work before you can write it for clients:

- Who is this for? Write for a specific person.
- Features vs benefits — people don't care about features, they care about what it does for them
- Structure matters — scannable content performs better
- Basic SEO — keyword usage, headings, the basics
- Content funnels — awareness, consideration, decision

Writing skills to work on:

- Hooks that grab attention
- Smooth transitions
- Calls to action that actually convert
- Your own voice and style
- Editing for clarity

And the AI stuff:

- Prompting AI for first drafts
- Structuring prompts for different content types
- Editing AI output so it doesn't sound like a robot wrote it
- Adding your own examples and insights

### Week 3: Pick Your Focus

Content writing is huge. Specializing makes you money.

Good niches:

| Niche | Why It Works |
|-------|-------------|
| SaaS/Tech | High volume, recurring needs |
| Finance | Good money, always needed |
| Health/Wellness | Evergreen demand |
| Real Estate | Lots of agents, high volume |
| E-commerce | Product descriptions, category pages |

Pick one niche and one content type to start. "I write blog posts for SaaS companies" is specific enough to land clients and broad enough to find work.

### Weeks 4-6: Build Your Portfolio

Create 5-8 samples before you start pitching:

- Write for your own blog or newsletter
- Offer to do free work for a company you like in exchange for testimonial rights
- Create samples for hypothetical businesses
- Be transparent: "This was AI-assisted, edited by me"

### Weeks 7-10: Get Clients

**Cold email that works:**

"Hey [Name],

I write content for [niche] companies that turns readers into customers.

Most content online is forgettable. I write stuff people actually read — the kind that generates leads.

One client saw their blog traffic jump 340% in 90 days with a focused content strategy.

Want to talk about your content goals? Book 15 minutes here: [link]

No pitch, just a conversation."

**Where to find work:**

- Upwork, Contently, nDash
- Apply to content agencies
- Direct outreach

### Ongoing: Deliver Great Work

Service tiers:

- Single blog post ($150-400)
- Content package ($500-1,500/month)
- Content strategy + writing ($1,500-5,000)

## Mistakes to Avoid

- Relying too much on AI — always add your voice
- Skipping strategy — content without strategy is just words
- Not editing enough — first AI output is never final
- Ignoring SEO — basics matter
- Underpricing — charge for results, not words

## 90-Day Plan

**Days 1-14:** Choose niche, study top blogs there, write 3 sample pieces.

**Days 15-30:** Create services and pricing, set up booking/invoicing, write outreach templates.

**Days 31-60:** Send 100+ outreach messages, post on LinkedIn, complete first 3 projects.

**Days 61-90:** Raise rates 20%, pitch one recurring client, build a waitlist.

## Bottom Line

This is doable. Content writers using AI are making $3,000-6,000/month within a few months. The key is just getting started and delivering good work.
