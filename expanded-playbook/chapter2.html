Chapter 2: AI Content Writing

Using AI as your tool to write faster, charge more, and deliver better content

Every business needs content. Blog posts, emails, website copy, product descriptions, social posts — the list never ends. Most business owners know they need it, but they don't have time to write it themselves.

That's where you come in.

This isn't about letting AI do all the work. It's about using AI as your tool while you bring the strategy, editing, and human touch that makes content actually connect. The magic is in the combination: AI handles the heavy lifting, you make it feel like a real person wrote it.

Why It Works

The content market is huge. Companies need more content than ever, but good writers are hard to find. AI makes you 5-10x faster — but the quality still depends on you.

Here's the thing: AI-written content often sounds robotic. It lacks personality. That's where you add value. You're not competing with AI — you're using it to compete with writers who aren't leveraging these tools.

Getting Started

Weeks 1-2: Learn the Craft

You need to understand what makes content work before you can write it for clients:

  • Who is this for? Write for a specific person.
  • Features vs benefits — people don't care about features, they care about what it does for them
  • Structure matters — scannable content performs better
  • Basic SEO — keyword usage, headings, the basics
  • Content funnels — awareness, consideration, decision

Writing skills to work on: Hooks that grab attention, smooth transitions, calls to action that actually convert, your own voice and style, editing for clarity.

And the AI stuff: Prompting AI for first drafts, structuring prompts for different content types, editing AI output so it doesn't sound like a robot wrote it, adding your own examples and insights.

Week 3: Pick Your Focus

Content writing is huge. Specializing makes you money.

Good niches:

  • SaaS/Tech — High volume, recurring needs
  • Finance — Good money, always needed
  • Health/Wellness — Evergreen demand
  • Real Estate — Lots of agents, high volume
  • E-commerce — Product descriptions, category pages

Pick one niche and one content type to start. "I write blog posts for SaaS companies" is specific enough to land clients and broad enough to find work.
